Privacy

A plain summary of how H Media Player handles your data.

What stays on your phone

Your settings, your recent searches, where you stopped in each video or song, your profile name, age, gender and photo, and the background picture you choose are kept on your phone. None of that is uploaded to the developer.

Files you download go to your Downloads folder. They stay there even if you uninstall the app.

What is sent over the internet

  • Search pages: when you search, the page of the search engine you chose opens inside the app. That engine's own privacy policy applies to what it receives.
  • Websites you open: pages and file links you open in the internet search are loaded from their own websites.
  • IPTV playlists: the playlist address you enter is requested from that address.

What we do not do

  • No accounts are needed to use the app.
  • The app does not upload your files, settings or history to the developer.
  • Private Mode clears web data when you leave the internet search.

Your control

  • Choose Default background in Settings to remove your background picture.
  • Change the search engine in Settings at any time.
  • Uninstalling the app removes the data it keeps on your phone.

Changes and questions

This page is updated when the app's data handling changes. Questions about privacy can be posted on the issues page.
