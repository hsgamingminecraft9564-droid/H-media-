Support

Answers to the questions people ask most. If yours is not here, report it below.

Installing

The download does not start

Try again on Wi-Fi. If your browser blocks the file, choose Keep or Download anyway.

Android says installs from this app are not allowed

Open Settings, then Apps or Special access, then Install unknown apps. Allow your browser or file app. The exact names differ on each phone brand.

"App not installed" appears

An older version may be installed with a different signature. Uninstall H Media Player first, then install the new file again.

Playing

My videos or music do not show up

Allow access to videos and music for H Media Player: Settings, then Apps, then H Media Player, then Permissions. Close the app and open it again.

A video does not play, or the picture is wrong

Some video types and codecs cannot be played on every phone. Tell us the file type (for example MP4 or MKV) and your phone model when you report it.

I do not see playback controls in the notification

On Android 13 and newer, allow notifications for H Media Player in Settings, then Notifications.

IPTV channels do not load

Check that the playlist address starts with https:// and that it is a playlist you are allowed to use. H Media Player does not provide any channels.

Search and downloads

Search results do not open

Choose another search engine in Settings, then Search engine. Some engines show a "verify you are not a robot" page inside apps. Trying a different engine usually fixes it.

Where are my downloads?

Files are saved to the Downloads folder on your phone. Open Downloads in the internet search screen or in Settings to see the list in the app. Tap a finished file to play it.

Report a problem

Post your question or problem on our issues page. You need a free GitHub account to post.

Please include:

  • Your phone model and Android version
  • The app version (Settings, then About)
  • What you were doing, and what happened instead
