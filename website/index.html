H Media Player

Your own videos and music in one dark, smooth player, with a mini player that stays on screen while you browse.

Download for Android

Android 8.0 or newer. Free. The file is large, so download on Wi-Fi.

All versions and release notes

What it does

  • Videos and music from your phone, with search, shuffle, and the length of each file.
  • Mini player like YouTube and Spotify: drag the player down to shrink it, drag up to open it again.
  • Internet search with the engine you choose in Settings: Google, Bing, DuckDuckGo, Brave Search or Ecosia.
  • Downloads from search pages, saved to your Downloads folder and listed in the app.
  • IPTV from a playlist you supply. Add only playlists you are allowed to use.
  • Your background: choose a picture from your gallery.
  • Private Mode clears web data when you leave the internet search.
  • Your language: choose it in Settings.

How to install

  1. Tap Download for Android above.
  2. Open the downloaded H-Media-Player.apk file.
  3. If Android asks, allow installs from your browser or file app, then tap Install.
  4. Open H Media Player and allow access to your videos and music when asked.
